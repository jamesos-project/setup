What's new.

Version 34
  • Games: Blackjack (with Hit and Stand), Fist bump, Dance, Cube Spinner, Keep Away, Roll the cube and Pop a wheelie, from a Games tab in Anki Archive.
  • My Vectors: one link that finds all your Vectors on your Wi-Fi, even when their IPs change. Add it to your home screen.
  • Turn each of Anki's features on or off in Anki Archive, under By himself.
  • Anki's 7 eye colors in Anki Archive's Settings.
  • Pet reactions, a welcome to Anki Archive, and fixes: the snow globe switch and an easier Smile back.
Version 33
  • Anki Archive: a second style on the phone page. He looks and acts like the original Anki Vector, with his own Anki Archive app on your phone.
  • Lost Anki: more than 20 things Anki built but never switched on, like laser chase, smile back, greeting you by name and the snow globe.
  • Messages: record a voice message for someone he knows, and he plays it when he sees them.
  • Reactions: tap one on your phone, like "Love you" or "Huh?", and he does it.
  • Anki's own settings on your phone: clock, temperature, distance, time zone and weather city.
Version 32
  • Say: type something on the Explore page and he says it out loud, with quick buttons like "Good night".
  • Mirror: his face shows what his camera sees, live, with the names of the people he knows.
  • Faces: meet someone new from your phone, see everyone he knows, and forget a face.
  • While you drive him in Explore, the camera shows who he sees.
