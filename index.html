JamesOS

Install JamesOS on your Vector

Custom firmware built on WireOS. It takes about 10 minutes, and he restarts by himself when it's done.

Already on JamesOS? Just change his Wi-Fi connects him to a new network and skips the install.

Free software under the GPL-3.0. Source code

What you need

  • A Vector that's already unlocked, running WireOS or another custom firmware
  • Google Chrome on a computer with Bluetooth
  • Vector on his charger, and this computer on the Wi-Fi you'll give him
  • Holiday themesHis eyes and back lights change with the holidays.
  • BadgesHe unlocks achievements as you pet and play with him.
  • SparklesGalaxy eyes, stars and rare surprise days.
  • Phone pageChange his settings from your phone.
  • Eye color and volumePick his eye color and how loud he is, right from your phone.
  • ExploreSee through his camera and drive him around from your phone.
  • PhotosSee, download and delete the photos he takes.
  • Phone page passcodeSet your own passcode, so only you can change his settings.

What's new in v31.2

  • Play tic-tac-toe against him on the phone page.
  • The phone page home is now easy-to-tap tiles in groups.
  • Pick one of his eye colors, or make your own, on the phone page.
  • Change his volume or mute him from the phone page.
  • Set your own phone page passcode, instead of the code on his screen.
  • Fixed: he could crash when you changed his eye color.
  • Fixed: holiday themes no longer cover up the eye color you picked.
